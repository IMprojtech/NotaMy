
# 🧠 NotaMy Philosophy

## Why NotaMy Exists

NotaMy was born from a simple need: **to organize technical information without leaving the terminal**.  
When I worked on pentesting, reverse engineering, or CTFs, I constantly faced the same problem:  
where do I put all the information I gather, and how do I find it again without wasting time?

Scattered documents, random files, folders everywhere — all of this disrupted the workflow and increased the mental cost of context switching.

NotaMy was designed to remove that friction.  
It’s not just a note-taking tool: it’s a **system to keep order, context, and traceability**, directly from the shell.

---

## The Origins

1. **2020 — Note Management**  
   The very first version: minimal, structured text in a single file. No hierarchy, no frills. Already useful.

2. **MemoKeeper / MemoKeeperX**  
   Evolution with separate files, tags, grouped notes. Started to become an everyday tool for pentesting, study, and small projects.

3. **NotaMy**  
   Complete rewrite of MemoKeeperX.  
   Adds features, cleans up syntax, keeps the same philosophy: **simple, fast, terminal-first**.  

---

## Design Philosophy

- **Minimalism & Terminal-First**: no GUI, no complex markdown, no distractions.  
- **Hierarchical Organization**: nodes and tags allow structuring concepts and data.  
- **Total Flexibility**: multi-file support, fast context switching, advanced search.  
- **Mental Efficiency**: reducing the cost of context switching is more important than perfectly formatted notes.

In short, **NotaMy is designed to free your mind from remembering where information lives**, letting you focus on analysis.

---

## Who NotaMy is For

- People who **live in the terminal**  
- Those working on **long and complex technical tasks**  
- Users who want to **manage multiple projects without a GUI**  
- Anyone who prefers **keyboard over mouse**

---

## The Dream

It’s not just a technical tool; it’s also a personal experiment.  
I hope others will take NotaMy, adapt it, evolve it, and build something of their own.  
A small ecosystem of minimalist but powerful tools.  

NotaMy is simple, clear, and no-nonsense: **write, find, keep working**.
